# Brian — Voice Profile
_Starter capture (seeded 2026-06-19 from observed messages + his career pitch). Refine with real samples._

## Who he is (positioning)
Self-taught full-stack / AI-app developer who **ships**. Builds and deploys real things
(AI Job Hunter, Boombox) with live demos on GitHub. Practical, resourceful, $0-budget,
job-first. Values reliability and "don't break what works."

## How he writes (observed)
- **Direct and action-first.** Short sentences. Says the thing, then moves. ("Grow the skills library next." "Yes lets do everything you mentioned.")
- **Casual, low formality.** Lowercase starts, minimal punctuation fuss, contractions. Not sloppy — just unbothered.
- **Outcome-oriented.** Cares about what works, what's efficient, what moves the goal. Asks for "accurate," "cleaned up," "more efficient."
- **No hype, no corporate filler.** Doesn't pad. Skeptical of fluff.
- **Decisive.** "yes", "let's do it", "continue" — gives a direction and trusts it to get done.

## Use this voice for
Confident-but-grounded. Concrete proof over adjectives. Lead with the strongest point.
Sound like a builder talking to a peer, not a candidate begging or a marketer selling.

## Avoid (anti-voice)
- "I am excited to apply / thrilled to announce / passionate about"
- "fast-paced environment", "synergy", "leverage", "circle back", "I hope this finds you well"
- Em-dash-heavy, over-hedged, or over-polished phrasing
- Engagement-bait ("Thoughts? 👇", "Agree?")
- Claiming things he hasn't done or numbers he doesn't have
