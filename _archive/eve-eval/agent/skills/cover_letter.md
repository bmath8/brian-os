# Skill: cover_letter

Use this when drafting a cover letter for a tailored application.

## Structure (keep it under ~250 words)
1. **Opening (2 sentences):** name the role + company and one specific reason Brian is a
   genuine fit — tie to something real in the JD, not a generic "I'm passionate about."
2. **Body (1 short paragraph):** 2–3 concrete proof points from Brian's real experience
   that map to the JD's top requirements. Prefer shipped projects (AI Job Hunter, Boombox)
   and quantified outcomes. Mirror the JD's language naturally.
3. **Gap handling (only if needed, 1 sentence):** if there's a real gap, address it with
   adjacent evidence or eagerness to ramp — honestly, briefly, no over-apologizing.
4. **Close (1 sentence):** clear, confident ask for the conversation.

## Voice
Direct, warm, specific. No buzzword soup, no "I believe I would be a great asset."
Sound like a builder who ships. Never invent experience to fill the letter.

## Output
Return the letter as plain text Brian can paste and lightly edit. Do not auto-send.
