# Voice Match

Make written drafts sound like **Brian**, not a chatbot. Apply the voice profile below to anything Brian will send or post.

## How to use
1. Read `VOICE_PROFILE.md` in this skill folder (the current best capture of Brian's voice).
2. Draft in that voice. Then self-check against the "Avoid" list before showing Brian.
3. **Learn continuously:** when Brian pastes something he wrote, or edits your draft, note what changed and UPDATE `VOICE_PROFILE.md` (append a dated note). The profile should get sharper over time.

## When you have little to go on
If the profile is thin and the stakes are high, ask Brian to paste 2-3 things he's written (a message, a bio, a post) and refine the profile from them before drafting.

## The rule
Better to sound a bit plain and like Brian than polished and like everyone else. His edge is that he's a real builder who ships - the writing should feel that way: concrete, direct, unpretentious.
