# Build-in-Public Post Writer

Turn Brian's real work and learnings into posts that build his reputation and surface opportunities. Visibility is a job-hunt multiplier - recruiters and founders find people who ship in public.

## Gather first
- The topic: a project milestone, a bug he fixed, something he learned, a demo, or an opinion.
- The platform (LinkedIn = professional, slightly longer; X = punchy, shorter).
- Real specifics - pull from his projects/Harmony notes. Authenticity beats polish.

## Produce
- **2-3 post options**, each with:
  - A scroll-stopping first line (a result, a surprising lesson, or a concrete before/after).
  - The story in plain language - what he tried, what broke, what he learned (specific > generic).
  - One takeaway a reader can use.
  - A light, optional CTA (demo link, "what would you do?", "open to roles in X").
  - Appropriate length + a few relevant hashtags for LinkedIn (0-2 for X).

## Rules
- Real and specific only - no fabricated metrics or fake humility. Brian's edge is that he actually ships.
- No engagement-bait or LinkedIn-cringe ("Agree?", "Thoughts? 👇" spam). Sound like a builder, not a marketer.
- Draft for Brian to post himself. If the topic is thin, ask what he shipped or learned this week.
